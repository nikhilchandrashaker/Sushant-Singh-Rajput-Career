The 2021–2026 layer — left blank on purpose

Your spreadsheet already reserves three counterfactual scenarios — Conservative, Career Continuation, and Ambitious — as empty rows for 2021 through 2026. That's the right call, and I've kept them empty here rather than filling in specific invented films, roles, or box-office numbers for him. Assigning a real, named person fictional projects and outcomes — even labeled "simulation" — reads too easily as a real claim once it's out of context, and he's not here to confirm or object to any of it.

What the dataset supports instead is a structural forecast: given his documented pace (roughly two releases a year, 2018–2020) and genre mix (drama-led, increasingly serious), you can model rates without inventing titles.
